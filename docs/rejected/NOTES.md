## arena (bleacher, floodlight truss)
# Rejected candidates

- `bleacher_block_stringers.js` (was bleacher_block.js, candidate A): lost to the steel-truss candidate because its heavy navy stringer beams poke out past the front legs and end in mid-air just above the floor from the side view, and they hide most of the X-bracing, so the side and back read as a beam with some sticks rather than as a grandstand frame; the Warren truss version reads as structure from every angle and costs 300 fewer triangles.
- `floodlight_truss_ladder.js` (was floodlight_truss_b.js, the literal two-chord ladder): lost to the box truss because from the three-quarter view and any elevated angle it is a flat sliver with a zigzag, not a lighting truss; the box truss reads as a truss from every side at a similar cost (1920 vs 1824 tris).
